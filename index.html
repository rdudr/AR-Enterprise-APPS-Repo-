Installing and updating

  1. Open this page on the Android phone and tap Download under the app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser: allow it for this one install.
  3. Sign in with the account your AR Enterprise administrator gave you.
  4. When a newer version is published, the app shows a message with a download link. Installing it over the old version keeps everything saved on the phone.

To check a download, compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one listed under Earlier versions and file details.
